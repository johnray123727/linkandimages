Typhoon Preparedness in the Philippines

The Philippines experiences many tropical cyclones every year. Typhoons can bring strong winds, heavy rain, flooding, and landslides. Staying informed and prepared can help keep families and communities safe.

Typhoon Safety Tips

Prepare an Emergency Kit

Keep drinking water, food, flashlights, batteries, medicine, important documents, and a first-aid kit ready.

Monitor Official Announcements

Follow official weather and emergency announcements instead of relying on unverified information online.

Stay Indoors

During strong winds and heavy rain, stay inside a safe building and keep away from windows.

Avoid Floodwater

Do not walk or drive through floodwater because it may be deeper or more dangerous than it appears.
